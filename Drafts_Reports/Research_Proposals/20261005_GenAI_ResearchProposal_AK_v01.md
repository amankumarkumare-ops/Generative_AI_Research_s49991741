# Research Proposal: Using Generative AI Tools - Boon or Bane

## [Draft v01 - 18 September 2026]

## 1. Introduction

Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and GitHub Copilot have rapidly transformed how students and professionals approach research, writing, and coding tasks. While these tools offer significant productivity benefits, concerns persist regarding accuracy, privacy, ethical use, and over-reliance. This research project investigates the dual nature of generative AI tools in academic and professional settings.

## 2. Research Questions

1. How do generative AI tools impact productivity in academic research?
2. What are the primary concerns users have regarding generative AI tools?
3. How does the frequency of AI tool usage correlate with perceived productivity?

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Quantitative Approach
- Online survey distributed to university students and professionals
- Questions covering AI tool usage frequency, productivity ratings, and concerns
- Data analysed using Python (pandas, matplotlib)

### 3.2 Qualitative Approach
- Semi-structured interviews with 8-10 participants
- Thematic analysis of transcripts
- Focus on user experiences, benefits, and concerns

## 4. Expected Outcomes

This research aims to provide a balanced assessment of generative AI tools, identifying both their benefits and potential risks, and offering recommendations for responsible use in academic and professional contexts.
